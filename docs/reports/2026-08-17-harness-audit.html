Reading the inventory — how the harness is actually assembled

Four layers, no coordination between them

  • Layer 1 — your own skills. 37 directories in ~/.claude/skills, of which 14 are symlinks out to ~/.agents/skills and 8 to ~/Dev/vdz-skills. This is the only layer you fully control, and the only one where deleting is trivial.
  • Layer 2 — installed plugins. 11 plugins from 3 marketplaces. Contributes ~38 model-visible skills plus commands and agents. Governed by enabledPlugins in settings.json — flipping a plugin to false removes its whole block from context in one edit.
  • Layer 3 — the claude.ai / desktop bundle. The anthropic-skills:*, design:* and cowork-plugin-management:* namespaces — ~29 skills — arrive with the desktop app, not from your config. ListPlugins returns empty, so these are managed in claude.ai settings, not on disk.
  • Layer 4 — built-ins. dataviz, artifact-*, code-review, simplify, loop, run, init, security-review and friends ship with the CLI. Not removable, and not worth trying.
  • The consequence. Layers 1–3 overlap heavily and none of them knows the others exist. Every duplicate below is a case of the same capability arriving three times by three different routes — which is worse than useless, because the model has to disambiguate before it can act.
